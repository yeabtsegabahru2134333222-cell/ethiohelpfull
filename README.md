# Ethiohelpful — Project Data

> Yep. Based **only on what you've told me**, here's the structured project data for **Ethiohelpful**. I'm separating your actual decisions from things you haven't decided yet so we don't accidentally invent your idea.

### 🎯 Core purpose

**Help confused Ethiopian high-school students figure out where to start.**

The site is built around students who ask themselves:

> *"What am I supposed to do?"*
> *"Where do I even start?"*
> *"Am I already too late?"*

The goal isn't to guarantee success or tell students what career to choose. It helps them understand their situation and identify realistic directions.

---

## 👥 Target users

**Primary audience:**
Ethiopian high-school students who are confused about their future or don't know where to begin.

The site is initially intended for **Ethiopian students**, rather than trying to serve students worldwide.

---

# 🤖 AI Assistant

The AI is the central guide, but **not the entire website**.

### Personality

* Professional
* Energetic
* Neutral
* Honest
* Useful
* Not unnecessarily discouraging
* Doesn't blindly encourage unrealistic goals
* Doesn't pretend to know everything

### Core philosophy

> **The AI doesn't take the leap toward success. The student does.**

The AI provides information, direction, reality checks, and possible routes.

### How it works

The AI first gathers enough information about the student, such as:

* Grade
* Interests
* Strong/weak subjects
* Goals
* Financial limitations
* Whether they want to study abroad or locally
* General location
* Other relevant information needed to understand their situation

It **doesn't need to interrogate the student for every possible detail**. It asks for additional information when that information is actually necessary to give useful advice.

---

# 🧠 Recommendation system

The AI works **strictly from the information the student provides**.

It should not randomly assume things about them.

It can suggest:

* One pathway
* Two pathways
* Three pathways

depending on the student's situation.

**Three is not a mandatory number.**

Every recommendation should explain:

> **Why was this suggested?**

For example:

> "This pathway was suggested because you reported an interest in mathematics, problem-solving, and technology."

The student can also see the reasoning behind recommendations.

---

# ⚖️ Reality checks

This is one of the strongest parts of your concept.

The AI should be honest when something doesn't match the student's current situation.

It shouldn't say:

> "You can't do this."

Instead:

> **"This path may be difficult given your current circumstances. Here are the challenges and some alternative ways you could approach it."**

The AI can suggest **alternative goals/pathways**, but it should still respect the student's decision.

The student ultimately chooses.

---

# 🛣️ Roadmaps

The AI can create a **long-term roadmap** based on the student's information.

You specifically don't want fake promises like:

> "Do these things this week and you'll succeed."

Instead, the roadmap represents **direction and milestones**, not a guarantee.

The roadmap can contain:

**Visual timeline**
→ Current position
→ Exploration
→ Development
→ Preparation
→ Future stages

alongside:

**Goals / milestones**

* Things to explore
* Skills to develop
* Relevant preparation
* Other appropriate steps

---

# 📊 Student dashboard

The dashboard is the student's **main control center**.

You want the biggest emphasis on:

### 1. 👤 Personal profile

A brief summary of what the AI currently understands about the student.

For example:

> Grade 11 · Interested in technology · Considering studying abroad · Exploring possible career paths

### 2. 🛣️ Roadmap + progress

The student's long-term direction and completed/incomplete milestones.

### 3. 🤖 AI assistant

Still readily available, but **the AI doesn't dominate the dashboard**.

The student is the center of the platform.

---

# 📝 Student history

Students can record things they've tried and what they learned.

For example:

> **Experiment:** Beginner programming project
> **Result:** Enjoyed problem-solving but didn't enjoy programming itself.

The AI can use these experiences **if they're part of the available data**.

Students can also:

* Change their interests
* Change goals
* Edit their profile
* Delete information
* Explain major changes in direction

If someone changes from one major goal to a completely different one, the AI can ask why so it understands the change.

---

# 🔄 Changing direction

The site **doesn't lock students into one path**.

A student can change their mind.

The AI should adapt to the new information rather than saying:

> "But you chose engineering six months ago!"

The student's profile evolves.

---

# 💰 Financial situation

The AI should consider financial circumstances because they can dramatically affect someone's available routes.

But you **don't want financial interrogation**.

Instead of demanding exact income, the site can determine whether the student:

* Needs significant financial aid
* Needs scholarships
* Has limited financial options
* Has greater flexibility

Financial information is used to make recommendations **more realistic**.

---

# 🌍 Local vs. abroad

The AI should ask whether the student wants to:

* Study locally
* Study abroad
* Or isn't sure yet

This matters because the available routes can be very different.

The site is designed specifically with the **Ethiopian context** in mind.

# 🌍 Philosophy

One of your strongest ideas was essentially:

> **Life isn't fair. That doesn't mean you give up.**

The site shouldn't hide difficult realities from students.

Instead:

**Show reality → explain the situation → identify possible routes → let the student decide what to do.**

# 🔒 Privacy / accounts

You don't currently have a server, so you don't want to build a complicated individual-account system.

The initial idea is therefore **local/browser-based storage** rather than requiring every student to create an account.

Students should have control over their stored information.
